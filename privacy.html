Privacy Policy

How BackGen handles personal information and website data.

Effective date: 30 September 2026

BackGen is the trading name used jointly by Ronald William Jennings and Kyle Bekker (“BackGen”, “we”, “us” or “our”). We provide website design and development, custom software, web/mobile/desktop application development, website maintenance, Google Business Profile services and SEO services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information when you visit https://backgen.co.za/ or contact us through the website.

This policy is drafted with South African requirements, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (“POPIA”), in mind. It is not a substitute for legal advice. BackGen should keep the operational arrangements described here aligned with its actual providers, contracts and practices.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

The responsible parties for personal information processed through this website are:

Ronald William Jennings and Kyle Bekker, jointly trading as BackGen

Germiston, Gauteng, South Africa

Email: info@backgen.co.za

Website: https://backgen.co.za/

For privacy requests, please use info@backgen.co.za.

2. Personal information we collect

When you use the contact form, you may provide:

  • First name
  • Last name
  • Email address
  • Phone or WhatsApp number
  • The service you are interested in
  • Your message or other project details

We may also receive ordinary technical information associated with a website visit, such as browser/device information, pages requested, approximate location information derived by service providers, IP-related information, referring pages and security/diagnostic logs, depending on the hosting, security and analytics services active at the time.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity-document numbers or other highly sensitive information through the public contact form unless we have specifically asked for it through a suitable secure process.

3. How we use your information

We may use personal information to:

We do not treat a contact-form submission as marketing consent or as acceptance of a project quotation or service agreement.

4. Lawful processing

We process personal information only for specific, lawful purposes and seek to limit collection to information reasonably necessary for those purposes. Depending on the circumstances, processing may be based on your request for us to take steps before entering into a service arrangement, performance of an agreement, a legal obligation, our legitimate business interests where permitted, or consent where consent is required.

POPIA regulates the lawful processing of personal information and includes requirements concerning purpose, retention, openness, data-subject rights and security safeguards.

5. Contact form and Formspree

The website contact form is currently submitted using Formspree at https://formspree.io/f/xlgkbnkl.

Formspree states in its documentation that form submissions are stored in the Formspree account and may also be delivered to a target email address or connected services, depending on the configuration. BackGen should therefore treat Formspree as a third-party service provider handling information submitted through the form.

BackGen should verify its current Formspree account settings, applicable privacy/data-processing terms, subprocessors, retention settings and any relevant international-transfer safeguards. This policy does not claim that any particular data-processing agreement or retention configuration has already been completed or confirmed.

6. Website hosting and Cloudflare

The website is hosted or delivered using Cloudflare services. Cloudflare's privacy documentation and service configuration may involve processing technical information for security, delivery and reliability.

Where Cloudflare Web Analytics is active, Cloudflare describes the service as privacy-focused and states that its Web Analytics does not use cookies/localStorage for its analytics collection and does not collect or use visitors' personal data for the service. Cloudflare also describes a client-side performance beacon that sends measurement data to Cloudflare endpoints.

BackGen will keep this notice aligned with the actual Cloudflare products and configuration used on the live website.

7. Google Fonts and future Google Analytics

The site's current stylesheet loads fonts from Google Fonts. That means a browser may connect to Google's infrastructure to retrieve font resources.

At the date of this policy, Google Analytics 4 is not treated as active merely because it is planned. Before enabling Google Analytics or another optional analytics service that requires consent, BackGen should implement a genuine preference mechanism with equally accessible accept and reject options, block the relevant analytics requests before consent, provide a persistent withdrawal mechanism, and update this policy to describe the actual measurement ID, cookies, retention settings, data sharing and other configuration in use.

BackGen should also keep advertising features, Google Signals and advertising-personalisation/linking settings off unless separately reviewed, appropriately disclosed and lawfully consented to where required.

BackGen will not describe GA4 as universally anonymous or universally unrecorded.

8. Disclosure to other parties

We may disclose personal information to service providers that help us host, secure, operate, communicate through or support the website and our business, such as hosting, form-processing, email or analytics providers, where this is lawful and reasonably necessary for the stated purpose.

We may also disclose information where required or permitted by law, court order, regulatory requirement, or to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

We do not sell personal information submitted through the contact form.

9. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside South Africa. Where this occurs, BackGen will consider the requirements that apply to cross-border transfers and the safeguards available from the relevant provider and contractual arrangement.

Because provider arrangements can change, this policy does not promise that data will remain in South Africa unless BackGen has verified that fact for the relevant service.

10. Retention

BackGen will retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legal or business record-keeping requirements, or as otherwise permitted by law.

Agreed operational deletion policy: unsuccessful enquiries should be deleted 24 months after the last substantive contact, unless a documented legal, regulatory, accounting, dispute or other lawful retention reason applies.

This deletion rule should cover relevant Formspree submissions, email inboxes, exports and applicable backups. The publication of this policy does not itself configure automatic deletion in Formspree, an email provider or backups, and no existing records are represented as having been deleted by publication of this notice.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risks to protect personal information against loss, damage, unauthorised destruction, unlawful access or unlawful processing.

Access to systems containing personal information should be restricted to authorised persons and security arrangements should be reviewed as the website and providers change.

If we become aware of a security compromise requiring notification under POPIA, we will follow the applicable notification requirements, including the requirements concerning the Information Regulator and affected data subjects.

12. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us about personal information we hold about you and may request correction or deletion where the law permits. You may also object to certain processing and may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise a privacy right, email info@backgen.co.za with enough information for us to identify your request.

13. Complaints

Please contact us first at info@backgen.co.za so that we can try to resolve the issue. You may also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa through its official channels if you believe your personal information has been handled unlawfully or your rights have not been addressed.

14. PAIA and Information Officer status

This page is a privacy notice. It is not a PAIA manual and does not constitute proof of Information Officer registration. BackGen should separately confirm which PAIA and Information Officer obligations apply to its business structure and keep the required documentation current.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, providers, legal obligations or data-processing practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
